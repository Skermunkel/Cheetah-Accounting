# Keeping records (and whether accounting software is required)

- **No law requires accounting software.** The law requires records: complete, accurate, kept in a form SARS can
  inspect. A tidy folder of statements, slips and working papers satisfies it.
- **Individuals**: keep certificates, receipts, logbooks and statements for 5 years after the return is submitted
  (Tax Administration Act s29), longer if a dispute or audit is open.
- **Companies**: accounting records for 7 years (Companies Act s24, s28); tax records 5 years after each return.
- **VAT vendors**: tax invoices, credit notes and import documents for 5 years (VAT Act s55).
- Electronic records are fine if they stay readable and complete. Back up the Cheetah workspace folder (an external
  drive or the person's own cloud storage) at least monthly.

## Habits that save the most time
- A slip or invoice for every business cost, filed in the inbox the same week, with a word on what it was for.
- Business and private money in separate bank accounts.
- A logbook for any vehicle used for business.
- Statements downloaded every month (CSV and PDF).
